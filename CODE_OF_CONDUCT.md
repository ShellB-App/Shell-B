# Code of Conduct

Shell:B is built by people who want their AI to be theirs. That takes all kinds: first-time contributors and
greybeards, people on a laptop and people with a rack, people who code by hand and people who pair with a model.
Everyone is welcome here, and everyone is expected to make it a place others want to stay.

## What we expect

- **Be kind.** Say what you mean, plainly and without contempt. Critique the work, never the person.
- **Assume good faith.** A clumsy pull request or a naive question is someone trying. Help them get there.
- **Make room.** Different hardware, languages, experience levels and ways of working are the point, not a problem.
- **Own your words and your code.** Including the parts an AI wrote for you.
- **Respect the license.** You are free to fork under the AGPL-3.0. You are not free to badger maintainers about the
  licensing choice.

## What we don't accept

Harassment, slurs, threats, sexualised remarks, doxxing, sustained hostility, or deliberately wasting people's time.
This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else the project is represented.

## Enforcement

The maintainer, [EveAeternam](https://github.com/EveAeternam), handles reports. Write privately through the contact
on that profile. Reports are read, kept confidential, and acted on, with outcomes from a quiet word to a permanent
ban. There is no appeal process beyond a second conversation with the maintainer, because this is a small project and
not a court.

Thank you for being here.
